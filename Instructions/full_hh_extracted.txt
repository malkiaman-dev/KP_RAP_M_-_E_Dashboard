
HH Survey Details:
Attempts: (radio buttons)Please select your attempt number to survey the householdLabel: attempt, Values: 1,2,3
These are the attempt through which we can find the attempt number.
Parent availability:
Mark who is available at the household (checkboxes)
Label: available values: 1=father, 2= mother, any other value means none of them
Remember; if both are available so it can have two values separated by space like 1 2.
available_1= father available 1=yes, 0=no
available_2= Mother available 1=yes, 0=no
available_3= none of them available 1=yes, 0=no
Unavailability reason
Why is the mother not available?
 1=Gone for work within the village
 2=Gone for work outside the village
 3=Lives in another city
 4=Lives in another country
 5=Have passed away
6=Other (specify)

Revisit:
For either father or mother or for both if:unavailable_reason is 3,4 or 5 so no revisit is required. Otherwise, we need to revisit.
mother_unavailable1= this variable will have the unavailability reasons for the respondent such as (1,2,3,4,5) as above unavailability reasons.
father_unavailable_other = if 6 is selected from above then this field will contain the reason enter by enumerator.
father_unavailable1: this variable will have the unavailability reasons for the respondent such as (1,2,3,4,5) as above unavailability reasons
father_unavailable_other: if 6 is selected from above then this field will contain the reason enter by enumerator.
Schedule time:

1,2,6) so we have to schedule a time for the interview:
So in the sheet schedule_spouse will have these time values on cell can have multiple values separated by space e.g 1 3 etcusing this will help us when to revisit for the respondents.
